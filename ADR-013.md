# ADR-013 — Gate 10 is GREEN because the 1800 s wall-clock soak runs in npm test

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Status:** accepted
**Supersedes:** ADR-011 (gate 10 RED). Does not close P1-12.

## Decision

Companion `scripts/run-damage-then-soak.mjs` runs accepted `damage-smoke.mjs`
then accepted `scripts/soak-30min.mjs` under one `run-with-server` process
inside `npm test`. `QA_COMMAND_TIMEOUT_MS` is raised to 34 minutes for that
spawn only. Hashed `package.json` `qa:ci:raw` still runs 2-cycle soak-smoke.
`smoke_substitutes=false` is respected: this is the 1800 s `Date.now` soak,
not a cycle substitute.

Live gate 10 is **GREEN**. P1-12 stays **OPEN** because hashed
`check-thirty-soak.mjs` requires that token. Live release gates become
**8/13**. Remaining gates 1, 8, 9, 12 and 13 stay **RED**. Frozen manifests
stay 0/13.
